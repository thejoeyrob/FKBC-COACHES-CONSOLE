FKBC Planner Tutorial

Build: Every section edits directly on the planner. Change category, description, coach and time without opening a separate edit screen.

Rounds: Use the Rounds button on a work section to split it into timed rounds and changeovers/rests. The section duration is calculated automatically.

Grading: Toggle Grading on a single section. Choose a belt and pick/type a syllabus technique; other sections remain standard.

Quick Type: Tap a technique/detail field and the strike buttons appear immediately above the on-screen keyboard. Change the visible buttons in Settings.

Timer: Full-body coach art enters on the left. Tap the timer background to hide or reveal controls. Previous, pause, restart, ±30 sec, next and End Lesson remain available.

Live edit: Use Edit on the timer and choose whether to pause or keep running while you adjust the planner.
