Privacy Policy

FKBC Coaches Console · Featherstone Kickboxing Club · Last updated: November 2026

Summary

FKBC Coaches Console is a lesson planner, round timer and team tool for the coaches of Featherstone Kickboxing Club. It has no advertising and no analytics or tracking software. Your lesson plans and settings are kept on your device. If you join the club's live features, your chat messages, saved lessons, timetable changes and library additions are also stored on the club's online database so the other coaches can see them.

What is stored, and where

On your device: lesson plans, settings, a cached copy of shared content, and your coach choice. Deleting the app or its data removes this.

On the club's online database (hosted by Supabase in London, UK, over an encrypted connection): when you enter the club access code to go live, the app creates an anonymous account on your device (a random identifier, no name, email or phone number) and records which coach profile you chose. After that, the following are stored there: chat messages you send (to the all-coaches room or to another coach privately), lessons you save, timetable changes, and additions to the shared library. Only coaches who have entered the club code can read or write this content, and private chats can be read only by the two coaches in them.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, health data or advertising identifiers. The only information held about you is the anonymous identifier, the coach profile you picked, and the content you choose to post, as described above. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for tracking or advertising.

Keeping and deleting content

Shared content stays in the club database until it is deleted. To have your content removed, contact the club using the details below. Deleting the app removes the copy on your device only.

Permissions

The app keeps the screen awake while a lesson timer is running and plays bell and warning sounds. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Sharing

If you choose to share a lesson or team brief, the app hands the file or text to the sharing option you select (for example Messages or Mail). Nothing is shared unless you do so.

Children

The app is a tool for coaches and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Featherstone Kickboxing Club – questions about this policy can be sent to the club using the contact details on the club website.
