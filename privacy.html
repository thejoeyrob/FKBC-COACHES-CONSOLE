Privacy Policy

FKBC Coaches Console · Featherstone Kickboxing Club · Last updated: November 2026

Summary

FKBC Coaches Console is a lesson planning and round-timer app for coaches. It does not have user accounts, does not use advertising or analytics, and does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

Lesson plans, saved lessons, library additions, chat messages between coaches on the same device, timetable changes and settings are stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage). We never receive this data. If you delete the app, or clear its data, it is removed. The Settings screen lets you export a backup file that you control.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect personal information, location, contacts, photos, health data or device identifiers, and contains no third-party tracking or advertising software.

Permissions

The app keeps the screen awake while a lesson timer is running and plays bell and warning sounds. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Sharing

If you choose to share a lesson or team brief, the app hands the file or text to the sharing option you select (for example Messages or Mail). Nothing is shared unless you do so.

Children

The app is a tool for coaches and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Featherstone Kickboxing Club – questions about this policy can be sent to the club using the contact details on the club website.
